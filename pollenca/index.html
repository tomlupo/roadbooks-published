Formentor mirador & beach

FROM THE DOORRECOVERY
18.8km
419m ↑
1h04at your pace
208m high point

97.0% paved · 99.7% quiet lanes · 0.1 km on N-roads

The gentlest introduction to the most photographed road in Mallorca: up the Creueta hairpins to the Es Colomer mirador, drop to Formentor beach for a swim or coffee, and roll home. In summer the Formentor road is closed to private cars for most of the day — you share it with buses, taxis and bikes only.

Port de Pollença → Coll de la Creueta (mirador) → Platja de Formentor → return

Go before ~10:00 for empty hairpins and soft light at the mirador. The beach approach is ~300 m of pine-shaded promenade path — roll it gently or stop at the road.

The full Cap de Formentor: mirador, the Fumat tunnel, the goats, and the lighthouse at the very end of the island. Constant up-and-down — 900 m of climbing hides in 42 km — and the return over the Creueta is where the ride bites.

Port de Pollença → Creueta → Platja de Formentor → Far de Formentor → return

Bring lights for the tunnel and start early — the cape bakes and buses multiply after 10. The beach detour crosses ~300 m of promenade path — gentle rolling, worth it.

Sa Calobra

FROM THE DOORTHE SIGNATURE
99.8km
1,953m ↑
5h28at your pace
717m high point

100.0% paved · 41.3% quiet lanes · 0.8 km on N-roads

Sa Calobra, from the door. Femenia and the high road past Lluc get you to Coll dels Reis; then the famous 26 hairpins fall 700 m to the sea — including the 270° loop of the tie-knot — and the only way home is back up. Ten kilometres at 7% with a thousand of your closest strangers. This is the day the whole book builds toward.

Port de Pollença → Coll de Femenia → Lluc → Coll dels Reis → Port de sa Calobra → climb back → return

Start at dawn: the climb is an oven and a car park after 10:30. Two bottles minimum — refill at the port.

Honest notes

  • Routing. Computed 2026-08-05 with BRouter's fastbike-lowtraffic profile over OpenStreetMap; distances and ascent from the router's geometry and elevation model. Short waypoint artifacts trimmed. Roadworks and closures it cannot know — glance at the line before you ride.
  • Surface & traffic are audited, not assumed. Every metre carries its OSM tags; the per-stage "paved / quiet lanes" line is computed from them. The Ma-10 and Formentor roads are cyclist highways by volume — quiet score reads them honestly as main roads.
  • Formentor summer rule. The cape road (Ma-2210) is closed to private cars most of the day in high season — buses and taxis still run, cyclists ride free. Check the current restriction dates on arrival; the lighthouse stage assumes it.
  • The GPX navigates. Turn cues plus café, water and bike-shop waypoints from OpenStreetMap. The maps are embedded down to street level and work offline.
  • Heat and water. The Tramuntana stages have long dry stretches — Lluc and the Sa Calobra port are the only refills on stage 11. Summer rule: bottles full at every chance, start climbs before 10.
  • Segments. Swept via Strava Segment Explore and your starred list; the named climbs were verified against climbfinder the day of writing. Sa Calobra's KOM chase is a lunchtime spectator sport.
  • Pace numbers. "At your pace" uses your Strava month — 25–27 km/h flat, ~90 rpm — plus half a minute per 10 m climbed. Sa Calobra day: add a fudge, everyone does.
